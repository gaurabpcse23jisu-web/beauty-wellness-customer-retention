# beauty-wellness-customer-retention

Week 1: Data Science Orientation & Problem Definition in Beauty & Wellness

This report focuses on identifying and defining a business problem in the Beauty & Wellness industry using data science and analytical approaches. The selected problem is Customer Churn Prediction and Repeat-Visit Optimisation for a fictional salon and spa chain operating across 22 outlets in Coimbatore, India.

The primary objective is to understand customer visiting patterns, identify factors influencing customer retention, and develop a data-driven framework to help businesses improve customer engagement and repeat visits.

The report explores the current beauty and wellness industry landscape, including digital transformation, personalised customer experiences, service diversification, customer behaviour and marketing trends. It also establishes the business relevance of customer retention and explains how data science can support better business decisions.

Several research questions and hypotheses have been formulated to investigate the relationship between customer behaviour, appointment frequency, service preferences, stylist continuity, promotional campaigns and customer experience.

The proposed analytical framework identifies important data requirements, including customer information, appointment records, service transactions, stylist details, product purchases, marketing activities and customer feedback. Key analytical variables such as Recency, Frequency, Monetary Value (RFM), average transaction value, cancellation rate and revisit intervals are also defined.

The planned methodology includes data collection, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation, cohort analysis, predictive modelling and model evaluation. Potential machine learning techniques include Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.

Additionally, the report outlines potential marketing strategies and an A/B testing framework to evaluate the effectiveness of targeted customer retention campaigns.

This Week 1 deliverable establishes the conceptual foundation, research direction, business objectives and analytical roadmap for the upcoming phases of the Data Science project. The ultimate goal is to translate customer data into actionable business insights and support more effective, measurable and personalised customer retention strategies.
